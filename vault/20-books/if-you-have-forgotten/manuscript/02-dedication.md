# DEDICATION

***

For the version of you
that may someday need
to remember.

For the girl who loved stories.

For the young songwriter
who believed that feelings
could become words.

For the woman who kept creating
even when the path ahead
was uncertain.

For every memory worth keeping.

For every lesson learned.

For every storm survived.

For every person
whose life touched yours.

For every person
whose life was touched by yours.

For every future self
trying to find the way home.

If everything else is forgotten,
may these pages help you remember
what was always true.

You were never important
because the world watched you.

You were never important
because of awards,
records,
or recognition.

You were important
because you cared.

Because you created.

Because you loved.

Because you tried.

Because you remained human.

And because beneath every story,
every success,
every failure,
every chapter,
and every reinvention—

there was always you.

And that has always been enough.

***
