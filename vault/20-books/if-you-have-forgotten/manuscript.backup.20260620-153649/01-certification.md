This document exists for one purpose.

To help you remember who you are.

Not who the world believed you to be.

Not who headlines described.

Not who critics praised or criticized.

Not who strangers imagined.

You.

These pages contain stories,
memories,
values,
lessons,
relationships,
and truths gathered from a life.

They are not intended to prove anything.

They are intended to help you find your way home.

If memory has been lost,
begin at the beginning.

Read slowly.

Take your time.

The person you are searching for
is already here.

You are more than accomplishments.

You are more than public perception.

You are more than mistakes.

You are more than praise.

You are more than criticism.

You are more than any single chapter
of your life.

The purpose of this book is not to tell you
what to think about yourself.

Its purpose is to remind you
of what was always true.

The girl.
The songwriter.
The dreamer.
The creator.
The woman.

All of them are still here.
