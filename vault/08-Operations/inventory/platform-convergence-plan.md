# Wonderland Platform Convergence Plan

## Sequence

1. Freeze direction.
2. Create capability registry.
3. Inventory current platform.
4. Assign ownership.
5. Consolidate duplicates.
6. Verify replacements.
7. Certify replacements.
8. Integrate Academy.
9. Archive obsolete components.
10. Purge only after certified replacement and rollback path exist.

## Rule

No direct purge before certification.
