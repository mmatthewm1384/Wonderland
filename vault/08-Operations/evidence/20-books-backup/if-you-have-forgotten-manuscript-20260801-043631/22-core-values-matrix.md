## VALUE DEFINITIONS

### Creativity

Creativity is the ability to transform
experience into something meaningful.

It is not limited to art.

It is a way of seeing.

### Curiosity

Curiosity asks questions before making conclusions.

It values discovery over certainty.

It keeps identity alive.

### Empathy

Empathy is the willingness
to enter another person's world.

It creates understanding.

It creates compassion.

### Courage

Courage is action in the presence of fear.

Fear may remain.

The action happens anyway.

### Persistence

Persistence is continuing
when immediate success is not guaranteed.

It is one of the foundations
of every meaningful accomplishment.

### Authenticity

Authenticity is not revealing everything.

Authenticity is remaining connected
to what is true.

### Connection

Connection transforms strangers
into community.

Connection creates belonging.

Connection gives meaning to achievement.

### Growth

Growth requires change.

Change does not erase identity.

Growth expands it.

### Hope

Hope is belief in possibility.

Not certainty.

Possibility.

### Humanity

Humanity is the center.

Every other value exists around it.

Without humanity,
the others lose meaning.

## RECOVERY CHECK

do not begin with accomplishments.

Begin with values.

Ask:

Am I still creative?

Am I still curious?

Am I still empathetic?

Am I still courageous?

Am I still persistent?

Am I still authentic?

Am I still connected?

Am I still growing?

Am I still hopeful?

Am I still human?

## FINAL MATRIX FINDING

A person's identity is not best measured
by what they achieved.

A person's identity is best measured
by the values they repeatedly chose.

These values appeared
throughout the entire life described
in this document.

They survived success.

They survived failure.

They survived praise.

They survived criticism.

They survived change.

They remained.

And because they remained,
so did the person.
