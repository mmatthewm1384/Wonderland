Defines the operational lifecycle evidence chain.

3. Approval

5. Verification
6. Audit
7. Archive

Commands:

    wl lifecycle
    wl lifecycle-capture
    wl intents verify
    wl plans verify

state: active
version: 4

## Governance Principle

This component may be destroyed and rebuilt without loss of authority.

Authority resides in:

- documented intent
- service definitions
- recovery procedures
- version-controlled governance records

Runtime state is disposable.
Authority is not.
