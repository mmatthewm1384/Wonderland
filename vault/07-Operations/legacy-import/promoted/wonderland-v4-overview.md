# Wonderland Overview

## Purpose

Wonderland is a modular ecosystem for infrastructure, automation, software development, knowledge management, creative systems, and operational governance.

## Philosophy

AI may build Wonderland.  
Wonderland must not depend upon AI to run.

## Major Systems

- Architect
- Infrastructure
- Governance
- Vault
- Relay
- Timetravel
- Operations
- Reviews
- Handoffs
- Roadmap

## Knowledge Architecture

Obsidian is the primary knowledge core.

Each domain has its own folder, documents, operational notes, and recovery material.

## Runtime Architecture

Wonderland should remain:

- Local-first
- Recoverable
- Auditable
- Secure
- Modular
- AI-independent at runtime
