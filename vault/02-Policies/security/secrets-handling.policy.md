# Secrets Handling Policy
<!-- id: secrets-handling-policy-v4 -->

## Rule

Secrets must never be committed to Wonderland vault, source, templates, logs, screenshots, or examples.

## Status

state: active
version: 4

## Governance Principle

This component may be destroyed and rebuilt without loss of authority.

Authority resides in:

- documented intent
- service definitions
- recovery procedures
- version-controlled governance records

Runtime state is disposable.
Authority is not.
