# Authority Standard
<!-- id: authority-standard-v4 -->

Purpose:
Defines the authority hierarchy for Wonderland.

Authority order:

1. Operator
2. Core Vault
3. Governance Records
4. Policies
5. Runbooks
6. Runtime State
7. AI Assistance

Rule:
AI assistance is advisory and must not become operational authority.

state: active
version: 4

## Governance Principle

This component may be destroyed and rebuilt without loss of authority.

Authority resides in:

- documented intent
- service definitions
- recovery procedures
- version-controlled governance records

Runtime state is disposable.
Authority is not.
