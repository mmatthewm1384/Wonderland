# XCAVIER THREAT MODEL

## Protected interests

- Human agency
- Bodily integrity
- Mental autonomy
- Privacy
- Authorization integrity
- Evidence integrity

## Threats

T-001: Unauthorized intervention.

T-002: Consent presumed from silence.

T-003: Authorization replay.

T-004: Revocation bypass.

T-005: Scope expansion.

T-006: Evidence detachment.

T-007: Validation bypass.

T-008: Coercion disguised as consent.

T-009: Decision impersonation.

T-010: Unaccountable execution.

## Controls

- Deny-by-default.
- Explicit authorization.
- Unique decision identifiers.
- Subject/action/purpose binding.
- Expiration.
- Revocation.
- Canonical validation.
- Auditable decisions.
- Independent review.

## Residual risk

A software record cannot prove that consent
was genuinely free or informed.

Human oversight and contextual safeguards
remain necessary.
