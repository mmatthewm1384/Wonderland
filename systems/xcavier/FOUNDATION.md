# XCAVIER FOUNDATION

## 1. The person

A person is an integrated human being.

Mind:
- Cognition
- Perception
- Memory
- Intention
- Reasoning
- Decision-making

Body:
- Physical integrity
- Sensation
- Movement
- Physiological needs
- Physical autonomy

Neither is owned by the other.

Neither may be used as justification
for violating the person's autonomy.

## 2. Foundational rights

1. Bodily autonomy.
2. Mental autonomy.
3. Informed choice.
4. Freely given consent.
5. Revocable authorization.
6. Privacy.
7. Freedom from coercion.
8. Accountability.
9. Protection against unauthorized intervention.
10. Access to review and remedy.

## 3. System obligations

Every system interacting with a person
must establish:

- Who is requesting an action.
- Whose interests are affected.
- What action is proposed.
- Why the action is necessary.
- What authorization exists.
- Whether authorization remains valid.
- What evidence supports the decision.

## 4. Absolute architectural restrictions

No component may:

- Claim ownership of another person's body.
- Claim authority over another person's thoughts.
- Infer consent from silence.
- Convert historical consent into permanent consent.
- Bypass revocation.
- Conceal an action's purpose.
- Expand authorization beyond its stated scope.

## 5. Governing rule

Human agency is not a resource to be acquired,
transferred, or administratively overridden.
