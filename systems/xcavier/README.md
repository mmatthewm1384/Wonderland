# XCAVIER v1

Human Agency & Embodied Integrity

Status: PROPOSED
Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose

XCAVIER protects the integrity, autonomy, privacy,
and safety of embodied people.

Its foundational model recognizes a human being
as an integrated person.

Mind and body are architectural perspectives,
not independent principals or competing authorities.

## Core components

### XCAVIER-CONTAINMENT-v1

Defines:
- Integrity boundaries
- Validation requirements
- Permitted information flows
- Authorization constraints
- Evidence requirements

### XCAVIER-ENFORCEMENT-v1

Enforces:
- Authorization decisions
- Transition decisions
- Revocation
- Denial conditions
- Decision accountability

## Governing principle

Containment establishes whether the conditions
are valid.

Enforcement determines whether the requested
operation is authorized.

Neither component independently executes
a protected physical action.

## Security posture

- Deny by default
- Explicit authorization
- Least privilege
- Revocable authorization
- Immutable decision provenance
- Fail closed
- No inferred consent
