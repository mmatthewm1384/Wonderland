# Wonderland Academy Governance

## Governing Documents

1. `academy/CONSTITUTION.md` — enduring principles and Academy Promise.
2. `academy/00-governance/manifesto/MANIFESTO.md` — philosophy and mission.
3. `academy/00-governance/charter/ACADEMY_CHARTER.md` — program charter.
4. `academy/00-governance/engineering-standards/ENGINEERING_STANDARDS.md` — engineering standards.
5. `academy/00-governance/assessment-guide/` — assessment and certification guidance.

## Hierarchy

The Constitution is the highest-level governance artifact. Other documents may evolve underneath it, but should remain aligned with its principles.
