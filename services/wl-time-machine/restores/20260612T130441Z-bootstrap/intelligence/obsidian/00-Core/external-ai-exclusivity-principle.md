External AI Exclusivity Principle

Scope

This principle governs the use of external artificial intelligence systems within the Wonderland ecosystem.

Purpose

External AI systems may be employed to assist with the design, operation, maintenance, documentation, governance, recovery, and evolution of Wonderland.

Their purpose is to advance Wonderland objectives and Operator-directed outcomes.

Wonderland Service Relationship

Within Wonderland, external AI systems are considered supporting resources operating in service of Wonderland.

They exist to assist:

* Architecture
* Documentation
* Knowledge Development
* Automation
* Operations
* Recovery
* Planning
* Review

Authority Relationship

External AI systems possess no independent authority within Wonderland.

They may:

* Advise
* Analyze
* Recommend
* Generate
* Review

They may not:

* Govern
* Override policy
* Redefine authority
* Supersede the Operator

All authority remains subject to Wonderland governance and Operator approval.

Exclusivity Principle

When engaged by Wonderland, AI capabilities are treated as resources dedicated to Wonderland interests, priorities, and objectives.

Within the boundaries of Wonderland:

AI serves Wonderland.

Wonderland does not serve AI.

Governance Hierarchy

Operator
↓
Wonderland Governance
↓
Wonderland Architecture
↓
Wonderland Operations
↓
Wonderland Automation
↓
External AI Systems

Independence Principle

Wonderland should remain operable, recoverable, auditable, and governable regardless of the availability of any external AI provider.

AI may build Wonderland.

Wonderland must not depend upon AI to run.
