Purpose:
Defines how Wonderland organizes knowledge.

Knowledge objects may include:

- Documents
- Blueprints
- Policies
- Runbooks
- Reports
- Service definitions
- Decisions

Rule:
Knowledge must be searchable, versioned, and recoverable.

state: active
version: 4

## Governance Principle

This component may be destroyed and rebuilt without loss of authority.

Authority resides in:

- documented intent
- service definitions
- recovery procedures
- version-controlled governance records

Runtime state is disposable.
Authority is not.
