Wonderland Operator Exclusivity Policy

Purpose

This document establishes operator authority and access control for the Wonderland ecosystem.

Operator

The sole authorized human Operator of Wonderland is:

Matthew Mahaney

Authority

The Operator has final authority over:

* Governance
* Architecture
* Infrastructure
* Security
* Recovery
* Documentation
* Automation
* Service Lifecycle
* Data Stewardship

Access Control

No individual, automation, service, agent, integration, or external system may be granted Operator status without explicit approval from the Operator.

Delegation of responsibilities does not transfer Operator authority.

AI Systems

AI systems, including Architect, ChatGPT, automation workflows, and future agents:

* May assist the Operator.
* May execute delegated tasks.
* May not become Operators.
* May not redefine governance.
* May not modify authority structures without Operator approval.

Continuity

Wonderland should be capable of operating without active AI assistance.

AI may build Wonderland.

Wonderland must not depend upon AI to run.

Amendment

Only the Operator may approve amendments to this policy.
