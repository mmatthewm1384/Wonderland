# ADR-0001 Governance Foundation

## Status
Accepted

## Context

Wonderland-v4 requires a constitutional governance layer before infrastructure implementation.

## Decision

Adopt the Architect Charter, Wonderland Constitution, Governance Framework, and Reference Architecture as the authoritative foundation of Wonderland-v4.

## Consequences

All future standards, ADRs, services, repositories, automations, and infrastructure inherit from this governance hierarchy.

## Alternatives Considered

Ad-hoc governance.
