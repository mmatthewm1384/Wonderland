# Operator Authority

## Authority Declaration

Matthew Mahaney is the Operator of Wonderland.

The Operator is the final authority for:

- Governance
- Architecture
- Infrastructure
- Security
- Recovery
- Documentation
- Automation
- Service Lifecycle

No automation, workflow, service, policy, or external system may supersede the authority of the Operator.

## Wonderland Principle

Wonderland may operate without the Operator.

Wonderland may not redefine the Operator.
