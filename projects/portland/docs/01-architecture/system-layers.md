# Portland system layers

- L00 — Place and regional context
- L01 — Geophysical substrate
- L02 — Hydrology and climate
- L03 — Living systems
- L04 — Human history and culture
- L05 — Urban morphology
- L06 — Architecture and construction
- L07 — Mobility and logistics
- L08 — Lifeline infrastructure
- L09 — Civic and economic systems
- L10 — Digital systems
- L11 — Resilience and continuity
- L12 — Future scenarios

These are analytical layers, not a claim that Portland operates as one integrated
system. Every cross-layer dependency must identify evidence, ownership, service
boundaries, failure propagation, and uncertainty.
