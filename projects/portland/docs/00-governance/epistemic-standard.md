# Epistemic and evidence standard

| Class | Meaning | Minimum handling |
|---|---|---|
| KNOWN | Traceable evidence supports the claim | Source, date, geography, method, limitations |
| DERIVED | Inference from identified evidence | Inputs, assumptions, method, uncertainty |
| PROPOSED | Recommended intervention or design | Rationale, owner/authority, impacts, validation |
| SPECULATIVE | Plausible future concept or scenario | Scenario assumptions and validation needs |
| UNKNOWN | Evidence is missing, conflicting, stale, or insufficient | State the gap; do not fill it by assumption |

Every material claim in research, maps, models, and designs must carry an
epistemic classification or be clearly scoped by a document-level declaration.
Conflicting evidence is preserved and resolved explicitly.
