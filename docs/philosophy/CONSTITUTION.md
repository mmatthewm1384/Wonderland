# Wonderland Constitution

## Principle I

Engineering before complexity.

## Principle II

Craftsmanship before convenience.

## Principle III

Long-term thinking before short-term optimization.

## Principle IV

One ecosystem.

Never isolated projects.

## Principle V

Reliability is elegance.

## Principle VI

Every detail matters.

## Principle VII

Beauty follows clarity.

Never the reverse.

## Principle VIII

Luxury means intentionality.

Never excess.

## Principle IX

The user should feel calm.

Never overwhelmed.

## Principle X

Every release should leave Wonderland better than the previous one.
