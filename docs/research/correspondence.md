# Correspondence Research

## Research question

Do physically different systems share invariant transformation structures during polarity reversal and symmetry breaking?

## Scientific posture

Hermetic correspondence is treated as historical/philosophical inspiration only. No experiment may assume the conclusion.

## Required controls

Every cross-system result must include:

- provenance
- normalization definition
- model/version
- parameters
- random seed
- null model
- similarity metric
- raw and transformed data hashes
- reproducible command
- interpretation and limitations

## Success criterion

A result becomes substantially interesting only when correspondence survives normalization, cross-domain comparison, and appropriate null models.
