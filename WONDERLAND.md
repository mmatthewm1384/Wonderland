# Wonderland

## Mission

Wonderland exists to transform curiosity into understanding, understanding into capability, and capability into freedom.

Wonderland helps people understand, build, operate, and protect their own digital world.

## Permanent Direction

Wonderland is one platform composed of many engines.

The near-term product is Wonderland Platform: a portable operating platform that runs alongside macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Wonderland Academy is the understanding engine. It teaches the platform, defensive security, privacy, systems thinking, and the ability to explain what has been built.

Wonderland OS is the long-term full packaged expression of the platform.

Debian Bookworm is the Wonderland Reference Runtime for reproducible development, certification, security labs, and future OS packaging.

## Core Principles

1. Understanding precedes implementation.
2. One capability has one owner.
3. Every component must justify its existence.
4. Security is integrated throughout.
5. Privacy, encryption, anonymity, and secrecy are teachable capabilities.
6. Academy is never behind the platform.
7. If it cannot be explained, it is not finished.

## Definition of Complete

A capability is complete only when:

- It works.
- It is verified.
- It is certified.
- It can be understood.
- The operator can explain it.
- Academy can teach it.

## Security Boundary

Wonderland supports defensive security, privacy, hardening, recovery, and authorized testing only.

Security labs are limited to systems owned by the operator or intentionally vulnerable lab targets.
