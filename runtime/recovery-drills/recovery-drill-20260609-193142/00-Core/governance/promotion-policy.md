# Promotion Policy
<!-- id: promotion-policy-v4 -->

## Purpose

Define when files, commands, services, or policies may be promoted into Wonderland core.

## Promotion Requirements

A promoted artifact must be:

- Documented
- Versioned
- Verified
- Recoverable
- Auditable
- Owned
- Rollback-capable

## Review Requirements

Promotion requires:

- Operator approval
- Verification command
- Recovery path
- Rollback procedure
- Clear dependency statement

## Rejection Rule

An artifact must not be promoted if it cannot be explained, verified, recovered, or removed.
